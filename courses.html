Art Classes FAQ

What age can my child start art classes?

Children can join the Beginner level from age 5 with no drawing experience. The Intermediate level suits ages 8+ with some drawing practice, and the Advanced level is for teens and adults who are serious about art.

Do you offer online art classes outside Hyderabad?

Yes. Online art classes run as live 1:1 or small-group video sessions, open to students anywhere in India, the US, the UK and beyond.

Where are the offline art classes held?

Offline classes are held in person at the Thevenkyart studio in Hyderabad, with hands-on guidance and access to a wider range of materials and mediums.

Do I need drawing experience to join?

No. The Beginner course starts from basic line control, shapes and proportion, so complete beginners can build confidence step by step.

How will I know how my child is progressing?

Parents receive monthly progress reports, and you can see real before-and-after examples on our Student Journeys page.

Can I try a class before enrolling?

Yes. Book a free demo class on WhatsApp and Venky will recommend the right starting level for you or your child.
