My Journey

From a software engineer to a full-time artist and educator.

The Beginning

I have always been fascinated by how colors interact with light. My journey began in school, sketching on the back of notebooks. Although I pursued a career in Software Engineering (working with SAP UI5 and Web Technologies), art remained my true sanctuary.

Balancing code logic with creative freedom gave me a unique perspective. I realized that just like coding, art requires structure, patience, and the ability to debug your own mistakes.

Thevenkyart Academy

I started Thevenkyart with a simple mission: To make art accessible to everyone. Many people believe they aren't "talented," but I believe art is a skill that can be learned.

Today, I have taught over 200 students, ranging from 8-year-olds to working professionals. My curriculum covers everything from Pencil Shading to Digital Illustration.
