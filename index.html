About Thevenkyart

"Art is the code of the human soul."

Venky blends engineering precision with artistic creativity, excelling in pencil sketches, paintings, digital art, and murals that range from expressive portraits to powerful abstracts marked by clean lines and deep emotion. His love for art began in childhood, sketching colors and light in notebooks, and grew through self-learning, creating murals for homes and restaurants, and drawing commissioned portraits. Viewing art as a skill that can be learned like coding, he founded Thevenkyart Academy to teach art in a simple, structured way. The online academy offers training from pencil to digital art for children aged five and above as well as adults, and has guided over 100+ students through easy step-by-step methods, personal mentoring, and monthly progress reports that build confidence and enjoyment. With a mission to help anyone express themselves through consistent practice, the academy emphasizes small classes, constructive critiques, and opportunities to showcase work, empowering students around the world to discover and share their own art stories.

His art career was born not from tradition, but from a relentless curiosity to learn new artforms. Recognizing that creativity is a skill that can be engineered, Venky established Thevenkyart Academy to demystify the artistic process, helping students transform from novices to creators.
